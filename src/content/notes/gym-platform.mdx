My scope was memberships and bookings. Stripe subscriptions drive membership status through webhooks rather than through anything the app decides on its own, so a failed payment or a cancellation upstream is reflected without a nightly job trying to reconcile two sources of truth. On top of that: online purchase of class passes and packages, capacity-limited class bookings with waitlisting and automatic promotion when a spot opens, and coupons. I also traced and fixed a connection-pool exhaustion problem. Separately, I refactored the ticketing platform's React Native point-of-sale app so the gym could take card and cash payments at the desk.
