A three.js refresher project: three holes on a fictional coastal links course, with pointer-based aiming, club selection, ballistic shot arcs and five camera modes (Tee, Green, Overhead, Orbit, Flyover). Every mesh, material and colour is generated in code — terrain is a hand-built `BufferGeometry` with per-vertex colouring driven by distance to each hole's centreline, and trees are scattered by rejection sampling into two `InstancedMesh` draw calls. Shot arcs are parabolas fitted to club carry distance with wind drift applied along the shot normal, landing via bilinear interpolation of the height grid rather than raycasting.
